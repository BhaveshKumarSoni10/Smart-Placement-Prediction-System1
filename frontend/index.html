Accuracy--

Overall test share classified correctly. Interpret carefully if classes are imbalanced.

Precision--

Of profiles predicted Placed, the share actually labelled Placed (fewer false positives).

Recall--

Of truly Placed profiles, the share successfully detected by the classifier.

F1 Score--

Harmonic mean balancing Precision and Recall into one score.

ROC-AUC--

Ranking separation between Placed and Not Placed examples across thresholds.
